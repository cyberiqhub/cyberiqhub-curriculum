
# 🌐 CyberIQ Hub

### Learn. Explore. Secure.

**Making cybersecurity and computer science education accessible to everyone.**

Welcome to **CyberIQ Hub**, a student-led educational initiative dedicated to providing free, accessible, and engaging technology education to students ages 10–18.

Our mission is to empower the next generation of innovators, programmers, and cybersecurity professionals by creating a welcoming environment where students can explore technology, develop technical skills, and gain confidence through hands-on learning.

Whether you're completely new to technology or looking to strengthen your cybersecurity skills, CyberIQ Hub provides the resources and guidance to help you succeed.

---

## Our Mission

At CyberIQ Hub, we believe that every student deserves the opportunity to explore technology, regardless of their background, experience, or access to expensive educational resources.

We strive to:

- Make cybersecurity and computer science education free and accessible
- Introduce students to programming, operating systems, and networking
- Encourage curiosity, creativity, and problem-solving
- Provide hands-on learning experiences and practical challenges
- Help students build confidence in technical environments
- Prepare students for cybersecurity competitions and future STEM opportunities
- Build a supportive community of students and mentors

---

## Our Courses

CyberIQ Hub offers beginner-friendly courses designed to introduce students to essential technology and cybersecurity concepts.

### Python Programming

Learn the fundamentals of programming through Python, one of the most widely used programming languages in software development, automation, and cybersecurity.

**Topics include:**
- Python syntax and basic programming concepts
- Variables and data types
- Conditional statements and loops
- Functions and problem-solving
- Hands-on coding exercises

### Linux

Explore the Linux operating system and develop essential command-line skills used in cybersecurity and system administration.

**Topics include:**
- Introduction to Linux
- Linux fundamentals
- Terminal commands
- File and directory management
- Basic system administration

### Windows

Learn how to navigate, configure, manage, and secure Windows operating systems.

**Topics include:**
- Windows fundamentals
- File management
- User accounts and permissions
- Windows security
- System administration
- Troubleshooting and diagnostics
- CyberPatriot preparation

### Networking

Understand how computers communicate and explore the technologies that power modern networks.

**Topics include:**
- LAN and WAN
- Routers, switches, hubs, and access points
- IP addresses
- DNS and DHCP
- Subnetting
- VLANs
- Routing
- Cisco Packet Tracer
- Network reconnaissance
- OSI model

## How Our Courses Work

CyberIQ Hub courses are designed to be interactive, beginner-friendly, and accessible.

Students learn through:

- **Live Instruction:** Tutor-led sessions explaining technical concepts
- **Presentations:** Structured lessons covering fundamental topics
- **Hands-On Practice:** Activities that reinforce technical skills
- **Practice Challenges:** Opportunities to apply concepts independently
- **Review Materials:** Quizzes and resources to strengthen understanding
- **Mentorship:** Guidance from student tutors and mentors

Our goal is to ensure students not only understand technical concepts but also gain confidence applying them in real-world situations.

## Getting Started

Getting started with CyberIQ Hub is simple!

1. Choose a course that interests you.
2. Open the corresponding course curriculum.
3. Follow the lessons in order.
4. Review the presentations and learning materials.
5. Complete any available practice activities.
6. Apply your knowledge through hands-on projects and challenges.

No prior experience is required for our beginner courses.
